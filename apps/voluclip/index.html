VoluClip — Privacy Policy — lizefield

Last updated: 2026-11-03

VoluClip
Developer: lizefield
Android: com.lizefield.voluclip

Camera, depth and audio

VoluClip uses camera images, depth information, camera position/orientation and capture timing to create and play 3D recordings. When audio recording is enabled and microphone permission is granted, recordings can include microphone audio. Availability depends on the device and app version. Titles, comments and tags entered by you are included in saved recording metadata.

Storage and sharing

Recordings, unfinished recording drafts, metadata edits, thumbnails, preferences and capture/measurement diagnostics are stored in app-managed storage. Import copies a selected .voluclip file into the app. Sharing passes a .voluclip file, including its recording content and metadata, to the destination you select through the operating system. Measurement reports can also be exported.

External components

The Android capture implementation uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Local recording storage does not describe all processing by that service. See the linked ARCore privacy information below. The iOS capture implementation uses Apple ARKit and platform audio/video services.

Retention and deletion

Saved recordings can be deleted after confirmation; unfinished recordings can be explicitly discarded. Closing or interrupting review can retain a recoverable draft. Removing a recording is not a promise that drafts, metadata-edit copies, thumbnails, measurement reports or copies already shared elsewhere are all erased. Local copies can remain until removed using the available app or operating-system app-data controls.

Common provisions

Developer and scope

lizefield is the developer and provider of the apps named on this site. The shared policy applies to lizefield apps that link to it, including existing apps not yet individually listed. Each named app page includes these common provisions and its own disclosures; a statement about one app is not a statement about every app.

Information handling and sharing

The information used depends on the app and feature, as described in the app-specific sections. Local processing or storage does not by itself exclude processing by SDKs, operating-system services or stores. If you export/share content, the destination you choose receives the exported content and handles it under its own terms. Revoking a permission stops access subject to the platform controls; it does not by itself delete previously saved data.

Retention, deletion and privacy inquiries

Saved local data can remain between sessions. The app-specific sections explain available deletion controls and their limits. Android app-storage clearing or uninstalling removes local app data; this does not erase copies already exported or data held by stores, service providers or support systems. Backup/restore behavior depends on the platform and app configuration; deleting an app record does not by itself erase separately held backups. For a privacy or deletion inquiry, use the contact form below; do not include unrelated sensitive information. The inquiry-content target below is separate from local app data and technical access logs. Deletion of records controlled independently by another provider is subject to that provider's controls.

Contact and external services

The PrivacyPolicy inquiry form sends your message, selected app, optional reply email, receipt reference and source-site information through Octerv's HTTPS inquiry endpoint to external communications and message-storage service providers used for receiving and handling inquiries. Providers receive and store that information; processing is not limited to this website or your device. Later email correspondence can create additional copies. This form sends no automatic reply email. Earlier contact forms could create separate form, spreadsheet, email and communications-service records; any copies still under lizefield's control are covered by the inquiry-retention rule below. Changing the contact route does not itself erase earlier copies. This site is hosted with GitHub Pages, whose hosting services and privacy statement apply to visits.

Protection and revisions

The inquiry form submits to an HTTPS endpoint. App-local storage and operating-system permission controls are described for each app. Exported copies, backups and records held by external services are subject to their own access and protection controls. The date above indicates this policy's latest content update.

Inquiry retention target and deletion requests

lizefield aims to delete inquiry content, reply contact details and related correspondence under its control around 90 days after receipt. If an inquiry is still in progress at that point, it is retained until handling is complete and then deleted. This is a manual retention policy, not a claim that automatic deletion or historical cleanup has occurred. It covers PrivacyPolicy inquiries, Octerv Setlist feedback and remaining developer-managed copies from previous contact routes where present. For a deletion inquiry, use the PrivacyPolicy form and provide the receipt reference or approximate submission date and app. Sending a request does not itself delete data, establish authority over another person's records or guarantee erasure from independently controlled provider backups. The inquiry rule does not set retention for app-local data or technical access logs.

Technical logs and separate retention

Website and inquiry hosting can involve technical connection records such as IP addresses, request information and user-agent information, separately from inquiry content. Octerv application logs use a seven-day retention setting. Log events are deleted through the service's retention processing after expiry, so this is not a guarantee of physical erasure at exactly seven days. lizefield does not retain additional load-balancer access logs for the inquiry endpoint under its current operation. Hosting and external-service providers may separately handle their own technical records and backups under their respective policies; those records are not assigned a seven-day expiry by this policy.
