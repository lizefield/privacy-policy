PrivacyPolicy inquiries — lizefield

Use this form for privacy questions, deletion inquiries or app feedback to lizefield. A reply email is optional; include it if you would like a reply. Sending does not itself delete any data.

Your message, selected app and optional reply email are sent through Octerv's HTTPS inquiry endpoint to external communications and message-storage providers used to receive and handle inquiries. A receipt reference and source-site information are added. Providers can store this information. Inquiry content is manually deleted around 90 days after receipt; if still being handled, deletion follows completion. Technical logs and independent provider records are separate. Do not include passwords, identity documents, payment details or unrelated sensitive information.
